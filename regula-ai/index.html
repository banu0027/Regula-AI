About Regula AI

Regula AI is a technology-driven solution designed to make construction and demolition waste management smarter, more transparent, and more sustainable. We connect key stakeholders and use AI-driven tools to simplify waste classification, recycling, transportation, tracking, and compliance.

Regula AI is a proposed startup and product concept based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu. Features shown describe the intended platform; no customers, partnerships, certifications, or approvals are claimed.
